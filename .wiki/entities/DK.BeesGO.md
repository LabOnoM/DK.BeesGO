# DK.BeesGO

[DK.BeesGO](https://www.bs-gou.com/DK.BeesGO/) is a digital knowledge repository designed to integrate both wet-lab and dry-lab experiments in a unified, interactive platform.

## Key Features

- **Bidirectional Linking**: Leverages Quartz to create a dynamic Graph View mapping connections between biological materials, experimental protocols, analytical data, and bioinformatics workflows.
- **Parallel Structure**: Mirrors folders such as `Nucleic Acid`, `Protein`, and `Lipid` under both `Wet/` and `Dry/` directories to ease tracing the transition from wet-lab bench experiments to dry-lab bioinformatics analyses.
- **ShinyApps Integration**: Curates a selection of outstanding community ShinyApps (known as `BSGOU-choice`, including `iDEP`, `ShinyGO`, and `Heatmapper`) for education and research.
